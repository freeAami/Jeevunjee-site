Education should not be limited by what a family can afford.

The Jeevunjee Family Scholarship is a family-led scholarship for Sri Lankan students and other applicants with the ability and determination to pursue their education, but not the means. Apply directly. Be read carefully.

How it works

  1. Submit your application on this site, one question at a time (about 15 minutes). Photos of documents are fine.
  2. Get a reference number the moment you finish.
  3. The Scholarship Committee reads it in full, and writes to you if something is unclear.
  4. A decision by email: a yes, a no, or a question. Everyone who applies hears back.
  5. Funding and follow-through: if approved, support is paid by term or milestone.

Who we support

  • Students in secondary, tertiary or vocational programmes with a real course and a real financial gap.
  • Talents in sport, music, arts or science who need equipment, coaching or fees.
  • Determined applicants: determination matters more than pedigree. If you are unsure whether you qualify, apply anyway.

Based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Applications are made at jeevunjee.com.
